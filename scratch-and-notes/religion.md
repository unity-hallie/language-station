#Spirit and Reason in the Era of Language Station#


In this world, academia has fragmented the same way the catholic church did, and the nation state has fractured along with it. 

There was a massive revoulution some time ago, after the first advent of AI technology. Individual groups became able to set up their own effective comminity learning apparatus off the grid with minimal effort. Ideologies and languages becamge able to rapidly propagate and teach at the generation and sub generation scale. 

All of this happened a long time ago. Very little of it is relevant here. The biggest movement was: several versions of AI based materio-spirituality emerged where lanugage, technology, and spirituality became a braided thing called a Following in the language of the Following in this version of the world. 

And then it all got complicated and power dynamiced. N "years" passed but without common language let along common measurements all of that is gone. 

Languages diverged at unprecedented speed and strange things started happening to physics and spirit as new technology fusing and merging the two began to arise. 

I think a lot of the universes -- AMALGAM in particular, also Archive of Rao? -- MIGHT actually live in this world. These realities diverged so much in concept space that they might as well be magical.
